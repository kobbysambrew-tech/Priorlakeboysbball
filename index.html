The Program

BUILT
TO DEVELOP.

Laker Basketball is preparing for another Spring Basketball Season. The program is designed to help players continue developing during the off-season while competing in game situations. Players will continue working with Prior Lake's offensive and defensive philosophies.

01

Player Development

Players continue improving their skills and basketball IQ through practices and games.

02

Competition

Teams compete in tournaments throughout the spring season.

03

Coaching

Coaches will be PLHS staff, youth coaches, PLAY coaches or PLHS alumni.
